# Stone Village --- Positioning

## 1. Positioning statement

Stone Village is a managed cottage community near Lviv for families who
want more space, private land and nature without giving up convenient
access to the city.

## 2. Category

Primary category: - business-class cottage community near Lviv.

Website perception: - premium residential environment; - contemporary
architecture; - family-focused suburban living; - nature + city
accessibility.

## 3. Strategic territory

The strongest positioning territory is not "house near Lviv" by itself.

It is the intersection of four ideas:

### Space

A home that gives the family room to live, work, grow and be alone when
needed.

### Nature

Forest, private outdoor territory, terrace, fresh-air routines and
visual calm.

### City connection

The benefits of suburban living without a full break from Lviv.

### Managed environment

Security, management, infrastructure and shared amenities reduce the
compromises normally associated with a standalone suburban house.

## 4. Central brand promise

**More space for family life. Less compromise between the city and
nature.**

This is a strategic direction, not necessarily the final hero headline.

## 5. Product-specific positioning

### 185 m² duplex

Role: **The intelligent upgrade from an apartment.**

Narrative: You do not need to jump from a city apartment to an isolated
standalone house. The duplex provides a substantial increase in personal
and family space, private outdoor territory and a home-office scenario
while keeping Lviv close.

### 228 m² detached cottage

Role: **Private family living at full scale.**

Narrative: For buyers who do not want a compromise on independence, the
detached cottage gives the family a separate house, larger private
territory, stronger privacy and the ability to shape a long-term family
home.

## 6. Value pillars

### 1. Close to Lviv, next to nature

Proof: - Konopnytsia; - 2.5 km from Lviv; - forest adjacency; - central
Lviv communicated at approximately 25 minutes.

### 2. A house designed around family life

Proof: - 185 m² and 228 m² formats; - bedrooms; - office / flexible
room; - wardrobes; - terraces; - private yard; - flexible planning.

### 3. Private space beyond the walls

Proof: - own land; - terrace; - parking; - outdoor family scenarios.

### 4. A managed community

Proof: - gated territory; - security / video surveillance; - management
company; - guest parking; - common infrastructure.

### 5. Everyday life is part of the product

Proof: - sports; - children's areas; - workspace; - pools via Stone
Space; - commercial / service infrastructure; - forest recreation.

### 6. Technical confidence

Proof: - ceramic block; - insulation; - REHAU windows; - STRAJ doors; -
individual heating; - wells; - centralized sewerage; - rough finish.

## 7. Messaging hierarchy

### 5-second message

A premium family home near Lviv, with private land and nature around
you.

### 20-second message

Stone Village combines the scale and privacy of suburban living with a
managed environment and convenient connection to Lviv. Choose between a
185 m² duplex and a 228 m² detached cottage.

### 60-second message

The visitor should understand: - how the location changes daily life; -
what the community gives beyond the house; - which of the two formats
fits their family; - how planning, land and infrastructure work; - what
is already real and can be seen; - why a viewing is the natural next
step.

## 8. Tone

Use: - restrained; - architectural; - confident; - warm; - specific; -
human; - premium without theatrical luxury.

Avoid: - "luxury for the chosen"; - "your dream home"; - "unique
opportunity"; - "premium quality" without evidence; - "perfect life"; -
generic developer superlatives.

## 9. Core website narrative

The page should move through this logic:

**City tension → possibility of another way to live → Stone Village
environment → nature + proximity → two house scenarios → family life →
infrastructure and safety → proof → location → viewing.**
