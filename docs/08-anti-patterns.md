# Stone Village --- Anti-Patterns

These are explicit prohibitions for design and implementation.

## 1. Generic AI hero

Do not use: - centred eyebrow; - giant centred headline; - two centred
buttons; - gradient glow; - three floating cards below.

Unless a reference explicitly requires it, this composition is
prohibited.

## 2. SaaS visual language

Do not introduce: - dashboard-like cards; - feature grids borrowed from
software websites; - product badges everywhere; - glowing UI; - fake app
screenshots; - tech-startup gradients.

Stone Village is architecture and real estate.

## 3. Cardification

Do not put every content group into a rounded white card.

Cards are allowed only when the information genuinely behaves like a
discrete object.

Avoid: - 3-card benefit row; - 6-card feature grid; - 12-card
infrastructure grid; - cards inside cards.

## 4. Excessive rounded corners

Do not default to: - 24--32 px radius everywhere; - pill-shaped
buttons; - pill labels; - rounded image containers on every section.

## 5. Decorative gradients

No: - purple/blue AI gradients; - random orange glow; - gradient text; -
mesh backgrounds; - blurred colour blobs.

## 6. Glassmorphism

Do not use glass panels unless a specific visual reference and content
need justify them.

No: - blurred transparent navbar for style alone; - floating glass cards
over every image; - excessive backdrop-filter effects.

## 7. Fake premium

Do not communicate premium through: - black + gold everywhere; - serif
font + huge letter spacing by default; - "luxury / elite / exclusive"
repeated in copy; - ornamental lines with no function; - marble
textures; - fake metallic effects.

Premium should come from art direction.

## 8. Repetitive section templates

Do not build the page as: headline → paragraph → 3 cards repeated ten
times.

Every major chapter should have its own composition appropriate to the
story.

## 9. Icon overload

Do not turn all USPs into equal icons.

Forest, land, safety, infrastructure and management do not have equal
emotional weight and should not look identical.

## 10. Render-only website

Do not create an experience where every major section is a CGI render if
real Stone Village photography exists.

Use real proof prominently.

## 11. Stock lifestyle

No generic: - smiling family in white clothes; - staged coffee couple; -
child running through unrelated stock garden; - business person with
laptop by a pool.

Use authentic project material or no people.

## 12. Copy clichés

Avoid: - "дім вашої мрії"; - "ідеальне місце для життя"; - "унікальна
можливість"; - "новий рівень комфорту"; - "простір, де мрії стають
реальністю"; - "життя, на яке ви заслуговуєте"; - unsupported "найкращий
/ унікальний / ексклюзивний".

## 13. Information dumping

Do not place all: - infrastructure; - specifications; - product
benefits; - commercial details

in the first two screens.

Use progressive disclosure.

## 14. Price-first identity

Do not make price the primary premium narrative. Commercial information
may qualify the lead, but it should not define the brand experience.

## 15. Identical 185 / 228 product cards

The two products must not be represented only by identical cards with:
image + area + bullets + button.

They need distinct storytelling.

## 16. Animation theatre

Avoid: - animation on every text line; - excessive scroll-jacking; -
long intro loaders; - cursor gimmicks; - 3D effects without purpose; -
elements that move just because they can.

## 17. Template spacing

Avoid identical vertical padding and section geometry throughout the
page.

## 18. Weak image treatment

Avoid: - random crop; - low-resolution background stretched full
screen; - text covering the architectural focal point; - images with
inconsistent grading; - too many small images competing at once.

## 19. Fake scarcity

Never generate: - "last 2 houses"; - countdown; - "only today"; -
inventory urgency

unless supplied and verified.

## 20. Invented facts

Codex must never invent: - prices; - availability; - deadlines; -
infrastructure status; - technical specifications; - guarantees; -
safety claims.

## 21. Reference cloning

Do not copy a reference page 1:1. Extract principles, not identity.

## 22. "Make it premium" shortcuts

If a design feels weak, do not fix it by adding: - darker background; -
more blur; - more animation; - gold; - larger radius.

First fix: - composition; - typography; - image choice; - spacing; -
hierarchy.
