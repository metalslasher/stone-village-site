# Stone Village --- Project Brief

## 1. Project

Stone Village is a suburban cottage community by Stone Development in
Konopnytsia, Lviv region.

The new website must not behave like a generic developer catalogue. It
should present Stone Village as a complete living environment for a
family: more space, private land, nature, privacy, safety, services and
access to Lviv without giving up the city.

## 2. Website objective

Primary objective: - turn interest in Stone Village into an on-site
viewing request.

Secondary objectives: - explain why Stone Village is an alternative to a
premium apartment in Lviv; - build trust in the project and developer; -
clearly separate the two current residential products; - demonstrate the
environment, not only the buildings; - answer the main questions before
contact with sales; - create a premium perception appropriate to the
product category.

## 3. Current product focus

### Duplex --- 185 m²

Two-storey duplex for a family that has outgrown an apartment but still
values convenience and proximity to Lviv.

Product role: - "upgrade from an apartment"; - more private space
without moving far away from the city; - own yard and terrace; - home
office capability; - family zoning; - two parking spaces.

Land plot for communication: 4.5 sotkas.

Relevant planning themes: - spacious kitchen-living room; - bedrooms; -
wardrobes; - separate office / flexible room; - terrace; - flexible
planning.

### Detached cottage --- 228 m²

Two-storey detached house for a family that prioritizes maximum privacy,
scale and independent private space.

Product role: - full private-house experience; - larger personal
territory; - stronger separation from neighbours; - family living at a
larger scale; - safe room / shelter-related advantage; - two covered
parking spaces.

Land plot for communication: 6.94 sotkas.

## 4. Location

Stone Village is located in Konopnytsia, Lviv region.

Key location messages: - 2.5 km from Lviv; - approximately 25 minutes to
central Lviv; - forest next to the community; - everyday city
infrastructure remains accessible.

Nearby points currently communicated by the official project: -
school; - fuel station; - supermarket; - Metro Cash&Carry; - Epicentr; -
Victoria Gardens; - central Lviv.

## 5. Environment and infrastructure

Stone Village should be communicated as a managed residential
environment rather than a collection of houses.

Project-level advantages: - gated territory; - security and video
surveillance; - management company; - guest parking; - modern children's
areas; - sports areas; - multifunctional sports field; - gym; - running
/ active recreation opportunities; - forest recreation; - workspace /
work areas; - commercial infrastructure; - pet-walking area; -
shelters; - access to two pools associated with neighbouring Stone
Space; - kindergarten communicated as part of the environment.

## 6. Engineering and construction

Confirmed/communicated project characteristics: - monolithic
foundation; - ceramic block walls for the 185 m² duplex; - wall
thickness: 380 mm; - insulation; - REHAU five-chamber windows; - "warm
installation" approach for windows; - STRAJ security doors; - individual
heating; - individual water supply via wells; - centralized sewerage; -
homes delivered with rough finishing.

## 7. Core strategic idea

Stone Village should not be sold as "square metres outside Lviv".

It should be sold as: **a more spacious, private and managed way of
family life close to Lviv and nature.**

The site should translate technical characteristics into human
outcomes: - land → your own yard, terrace and privacy; - forest → daily
walks, quiet mornings, cleaner visual environment; - office → ability to
work from home without occupying family space; - two parking spaces → no
daily parking friction; - gated territory → more controlled family
environment; - management company → less household operational
friction; - infrastructure → more daily needs handled near home; -
flexible planning → house adapts to the family; - shelter / safe room →
security is designed into the living environment.

## 8. Main proof categories

The new website should rely on proof, not only promises: - real
completed buildings; - real streets and landscaping; - real residents /
lived-in phases if available; - construction progress; - infrastructure
that already exists; - house plans; - masterplan; - real forest
adjacency; - developer track record; - virtual tour; - technical
specifications; - status of each current product.

## 9. Website success criterion

A visitor should leave the first part of the page understanding: 1. what
Stone Village is; 2. why it is meaningfully different from an apartment;
3. that it is close to Lviv; 4. that nature and privacy are central to
the experience; 5. that there are two relevant house formats --- 185 m²
duplex and 228 m² detached cottage; 6. that the logical next step is an
on-site viewing.

## 10. Source discipline

Do not invent: - availability; - current prices; - discounts; -
construction deadlines; - number of remaining houses; - infrastructure
completion status; - legal claims about shelter protection; - exact
product specifications not present in verified project materials.

Any dynamic commercial data should be supplied separately before launch.
