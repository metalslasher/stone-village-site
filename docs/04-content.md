## 1. Content principle

The website should sell the lived experience first and explain
specifications second.

Every important feature should answer: **"What changes in the family's
everyday life because of this?"**

## 2. Core story themes

### Theme A --- Space that changes family life

Do not say only: - 185 m²; - 228 m².

Translate into: - separate work and family zones; - rooms that can
change function; - private bedrooms; - wardrobes and storage; -
kitchen-living room as the social centre; - terrace as an extension of
the house; - enough space to live together without constantly being in
each other's way.

### Theme B --- Your own outside space

Feature: - private land; - yard; - terrace.

Human outcome: - breakfast outside; - children's play; - dinner with
friends; - dog outside; - small garden; - quiet evening without leaving
home.

### Theme C --- Forest as part of the routine

Feature: - forest next to the community.

Human outcome: - morning walk; - running; - walks with children; - dog
walks; - visual calm; - nature is not a weekend destination.

### Theme D --- Lviv remains close

Feature: - 2.5 km from Lviv; - central Lviv around 25 minutes.

Human outcome: - work, meetings, restaurants and city life remain
available; - suburban life does not equal isolation.

### Theme E --- Managed environment

Feature: - gated territory; - surveillance; - management company; -
guest parking.

Human outcome: - less daily friction; - more predictable common space; -
the environment is maintained as a system.

### Theme F --- Family infrastructure

Feature: - children's spaces; - sports; - workspace; - pools; -
commercial amenities; - kindergarten; - recreation.

Human outcome: - more of family life happens near home; - children and
adults have separate reasons to enjoy the environment.

### Theme G --- Safety

Feature: - controlled territory; - shelters; - safe-room context for the
228 m² product where verified.

Human outcome: - safety is treated as part of the residential concept,
not an afterthought.

## 3. Product content

### Duplex 185 m²

Suggested content framing: **More than an apartment. Easier than an
isolated house.**

Explain: - two-storey family zoning; - 4.5 sotkas of private land; -
yard and terrace; - home office / flexible room; - two parking spaces; -
flexible planning; - construction and energy-efficiency features.

### Detached cottage 228 m²

Suggested content framing: **A separate home for a family that wants
maximum private space.**

Explain: - detached house; - 6.94 sotkas of land; - private outdoor
territory; - covered parking for two cars; - flexible planning; - safe
room / shelter feature using only legally approved wording; - access to
the wider Stone Village / Stone Space environment.

## 4. Proof content

Prioritize: - real photography; - completed phases; - occupied /
lived-in environment where available; - construction progress; - actual
infrastructure; - plans; - masterplan; - technical details; - virtual
tour; - developer evidence.

Use renders as: - explanation of future state; - spatial
visualization; - support for sections where real photography is
unavailable.

Do not let renders dominate the entire experience if real material
exists.

## 5. CTA language

Primary: **Записатись на перегляд**

Secondary CTA candidates: - Переглянути планування - Обрати формат
будинку - Отримати консультацію - Переглянути генплан

Avoid excessive CTA variants. The page should have one clear primary
action.

## 6. Content rhythm

Alternate: - emotional / visual section; - factual section; - product
section; - lifestyle proof; - technical proof; - conversion point.

Do not create ten consecutive sections that all look like: headline +
paragraph + 3 cards.

## 7. Copy style

Headlines: - short; - specific; - conceptual; - not overloaded with
adjectives.

Body: - concrete; - calm; - explain benefits through scenarios; - use
technical detail where it increases confidence.

Labels: - functional and concise.

## 8. Content that should not be hardcoded without verification

-   current price;
-   current discount;
-   exact remaining inventory;
-   construction completion dates;
-   "last house" scarcity;
-   financing terms;
-   infrastructure opening dates;
-   legally sensitive safety guarantees.
