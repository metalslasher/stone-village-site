# Stone Village --- Reference Analysis

## Reference strategy

References are not templates to copy.

For every reference, extract: 1. what problem it solves well; 2. which
visual principle is useful; 3. what should NOT be copied; 4. where that
principle could appear in Stone Village.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Montana City

URL: https://www.montana.city/

### Why it is relevant

Closest reference in product logic: - cottage community; - forest; -
city proximity; - family infrastructure; - multiple house formats; -
safety; - managed environment.

### What to take

**Storytelling before catalogue.** The site begins by framing the
conflict between city opportunity and the desire for privacy, safety and
autonomy before going deep into product.

**Lifestyle translation.** Forest is explained through actual scenarios:
running, walking the dog, family walks --- not only "3 minutes to
forest".

**Infrastructure as a system.** Amenities are described as part of
everyday life and supported by walking-time logic.

**Proof.** Construction progress and already-built houses help reduce
the "render-only project" feeling.

**Separate product catalogue.** The main narrative is not overloaded by
all product details.

### Apply to Stone Village

-   use the city-vs-space tension;
-   translate forest and infrastructure into family routines;
-   make proof a major part of the page;
-   keep product details deep enough to convert but do not turn the hero
    into a catalogue.

### Do not copy

-   exact copy structure;
-   exact animations;
-   "15-minute city" concept unless it is genuinely applicable;
-   Montana's visual identity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Horizonte Village

URL: https://horizonte-village.com/

### Why it is relevant

Useful as an art-direction reference for premium residential
presentation.

### What to take

**Large-scale visual confidence.** The experience lets architecture and
landscape carry the premium perception.

**Minimal first-screen communication.** The project is introduced with a
restrained amount of text rather than a dense developer offer.

**Editorial luxury.** Premium is communicated through scale,
photography, typography and space --- not through badges, gold gradients
or excessive decoration.

### Apply to Stone Village

-   hero should feel architectural and cinematic;
-   use fewer but stronger elements;
-   allow large negative space;
-   avoid clutter around premium imagery.

### Do not copy

-   Mediterranean luxury cues that do not belong to Lviv / Konopnytsia;
-   excessive "luxury" language;
-   visual treatments unsupported by Stone Village assets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. PROMIN

URL: https://promin.town/

### Why it is relevant

Ukrainian cottage-community reference with: - private territory; -
infrastructure; - two-car scenario; - outdoor living; - shelter /
safe-room communication.

### What to take

**Concrete infrastructure.** The project quickly makes the environment
tangible.

**House planning explained through use.** Kitchen-living room, terrace,
parking and yard are connected to how the house works.

**Safety is not hidden.** The safe-room concept is given dedicated
attention.

### Apply to Stone Village

-   explain infrastructure concretely;
-   give safety its own meaningful section;
-   make house planning understandable through family scenarios.

### Do not copy

-   information-heavy presentation as the main premium visual language;
-   repetitive bullet-led sections;
-   generic developer layout patterns.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Piemont

URL: https://piemont.com.ua/

### Role in the reference pack

Use primarily as a Ukrainian premium cottage-market benchmark.

During visual implementation, collect screenshots from: - hero; -
typography; - house presentation; - gallery; - transitions; - CTA /
forms; - mobile.

### Apply to Stone Village

Use only the specific visual solutions that strengthen: - status; -
architectural presentation; - restrained premium feel; - house /
landscape integration.

Do not copy the site's identity as a whole.

# Cross-reference synthesis

## Hero

Primary inspiration: - Horizonte Village for scale and restraint; -
Montana City for clear lifestyle positioning.

Stone Village target: **cinematic architecture/nature + one strong
positioning thought + one primary CTA.**

## Storytelling

Primary inspiration: - Montana City.

Stone Village target: move from apartment/city tension into a better
family-life scenario.

## Infrastructure

Primary inspiration: - Montana City + PROMIN.

Stone Village target: do not show infrastructure as 12 equal icons.
Group it into: - family; - active life; - work; - safety; - service.

## Product presentation

Target: more editorial than PROMIN; more conversion-focused than
Horizonte; clearer separation of 185 vs 228 than the current Stone
Village page.

## Premium feeling

Primary inspiration: - Horizonte Village; - selected Piemont visual
patterns.

Premium must come from: - typography; - scale; - image quality; -
rhythm; - restraint; - motion; - art direction.

Not from: - gold; - black backgrounds everywhere; - glassmorphism; -
luxury clichés.

## Proof

Primary inspiration: - Montana City.

Stone Village should visibly show: - what is already built; - real
environment; - construction; - infrastructure; - plans / masterplan.

## Reference usage rule for Codex

Never write: "Make the site like Montana."

Instead: "Use the narrative sequencing principle identified in
06-reference-analysis.md while preserving the Stone Village design
system in 07-design.md."

The design system has priority over any single reference.
